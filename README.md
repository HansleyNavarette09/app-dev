# Heneral Luna

> "Bayan o sarili?"

---

## Introduction

**Heneral Luna** is a Filipino historical war film about General Antonio Luna one of the bravest commanders of the Philippine Revolutionary Army The movie was directed by Jerrold Tarog and tells the story of Luna's role during the Philippine-American War

- **Genre:** Historical drama, war
- **Year:** 2015

## Synopsis

The story takes place in 1898 to 1899 when the Philippines is fighting to defend its newly declared independence from American forces General Antonio Luna is a military leader who wants to build a disciplined, professional army. luna becomes frustrated because many of the country's leaders care more about their own interests than about the nation Luna clashes with other officials, including some within President Emilio Aguinaldo His strict methods earn him respect from some and make enemies of others.The film ends with a tragic event that shows the high cost of disunity.

## Characters

1. **Antonio Luna** – The passionate and strict general who leads the army.
2. **Emilio Aguinaldo** – The President of the First Philippine Republic.
3. **Felipe Buencamino** – A politician who often opposes Luna's views.
4. **Apolinario Mabini** – An advisor to Aguinaldo, known as the "Sublime Paralytic."
5. **Joven Hernando** – A young soldier who serves under Luna.

## Personal Review

I chose this movie because it teaches Philippine history in a way that feels exciting and relevant today.

### Things I liked

- The acting, especially the powerful performance of the lead actor.
- The story's message about putting the country before personal interests.
- The mix of serious history with moments of humor.

**My rating:** 10/10

**Recommendation:** I recommend it to everyone especially students and anyone who wants to understand the Philippine-American War.
